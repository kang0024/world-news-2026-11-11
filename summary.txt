11 November 2026

Iran: Trump said the war with Iran is "militarily over", but Tehran said the Strait of Hormuz stays shut until seven conditions are met; the Revolutionary Guard reported an oil tanker ablaze after hitting a mine, and Brent crude fell below $90 a barrel (World Watch).

Ukraine: US, Russian and Ukrainian delegations began a second day of talks in Abu Dhabi, the first in that format since February; Rubio called them "very productive" and Zelenskyy "constructive", while long-range strikes went on (Xinhua, AP).

Climate: leaders opened the COP31 summit in Antalya, where Türkiye wants electricity to reach 35% of global energy use by 2035; Erdogan, Guterres and Lula were due to speak (Anadolu).

Israel: the new Knesset was sworn in and opposition parties filed bills to repeal Netanyahu's judicial overhaul and open an inquiry into 7 October 2023; Eisenkot's bloc holds 48 seats to 53 for Netanyahu's allies, short of 61, and has six weeks to form a government (Washington Post).

US: consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above the 0.3% forecast, and the BLS gave no annual rate because the 2025 shutdown stopped its survey data; the House voted 211-204 to shelve a resolution to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth (AFP).

Russia: Novaya Gazeta, the last big independent newspaper, closed its Moscow newsroom after a foreign-agent label, a refused lease and blocked donations (NYT, DW).

Gulf: Yemen's Houthis said they fired missiles and drones at Riyadh's King Khalid airport, pumping stations on Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline and a financial centre (Xinhua).

Europe: eleven people died and 50 were hurt in two pile-ups on Bulgaria's Trakia motorway near Yambol (Xinhua).

Africa: Congo's Ebola outbreak has 10,668 confirmed cases, new infections centred on North Kivu, the WHO said (World Watch).

Asia: Japan will give the Philippines a coastal radar and unmanned aircraft under a security aid deal as both face China (AP).
