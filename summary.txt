11 November 2026

US-brokered talks between Russia and Ukraine ended a second day in Abu Dhabi after overnight drone exchanges; Baltic states issued shelter alerts and Russia said it downed 146 drones (AP, Al Jazeera). Volodymyr Zelenskyy said he was "happy" with US mediators' changed approach.

The Republican-led US House voted 211-204 to send an impeachment resolution against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to the Judiciary Committee, ending it; Trump called him a "patriot" (AFP).

Gaza's civil defence said Israeli strikes killed at least seven people, a teenager among them, hitting displacement camps. Israeli aircraft struck Kfar Tebnit in southern Lebanon, where UN peacekeepers reported being fired on (The National, AFP).

Iran's president, Masoud Pezeshkian, accused Washington of wielding "the mother of all bombs" as diplomacy, after the US first used a PrSM missile at Lamerd; Qatar's prime minister joined G7 talks on Iran (AFP).

US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above the 0.3% forecast, with core inflation up 0.3% for a third month, keeping the Federal Reserve on course to raise rates in December (Reuters, BLS). Wall Street fell, Nvidia down 5.5%; Europe rose on tech results; the Sensex gained 0.95% to 81,879.40 and Hong Kong's Hang Seng fell 0.98% to 24,315.

A week after the midterms, both chambers' control was undecided; recounts continued in Florida's 27th district and South Carolina, where Republican Maria Elvira Salazar won (AP).

Japan will give the Philippines a coastal radar system and unmanned aircraft under a security aid deal as both face tensions with China (AP).

At COP31 in Antalya finance talks ran late; an electrification pledge drew criticism for not requiring clean or renewable power, and UN Women counted 1.5 billion women and girls in climate hotspots (ABC News, IPS).

The UN said nearly 64,000 people fled Nigeria for Niger, and South Sudan's first elections are six weeks away; drone strikes are routine in Sudan's war (UN News).
